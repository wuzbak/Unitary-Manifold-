What Lean4 Verifies

The Lean4 files verify algebraic kernel constraints for non-perturbative problems — not full functional-space proofs. Each "proved" theorem is a necessary algebraic condition on the operator algebra, not a complete non-perturbative closure. This distinction is documented honestly in every Lean4 file.

WHAT IS PROVED
  • Arithmetic consistency of operator algebra
  • KK correction coefficients (n_w/k_CS fractions)
  • Parity conditions from n_w = 5 (odd)
  • Braid identity 5² + 7² = 74
  • Algebraic kernel constraints (necessary conditions)
WHAT IS NOT PROVED
  • Full Wheeler-DeWitt functional quantization
  • Non-perturbative operator-ordering uniqueness
  • Physical Hilbert-space inner product
  • Complete closed-form functional solutions
