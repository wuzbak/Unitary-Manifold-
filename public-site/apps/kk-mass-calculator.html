Physics Notes

The KK graviton mass sets from RS1 geometry: mG,KK = k̃ × x1 × √(k_CS/n_w). The UM theoretical lower bound is 5.0 TeV from the Pillar 435 HL-LHC preregistration. Both ATLAS and CMS Run 2 bounds are below this — the theory is consistent with all current collider data.

Decision window: HL-LHC Run 4 (2029–2033) can reach ~6.5 TeV at k̃ = 0.10 with 3000 fb⁻¹.
