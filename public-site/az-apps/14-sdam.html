OFDM-over-Audio Spec Sheet

Channel parameters

Nominal acoustic range

~10 m at 4000 Hz
~30 m at 1000 Hz
Higher carriers trade reach for throughput.

Encoding model

UTF-8 bytes are rendered as hex, then mapped to sine segments. Each unique character value creates one visualized cycle with f = carrier + (charCode × 20).

Ideal decode assumptions

This simulator assumes deterministic sync, no packet loss, and zero symbol errors. BER stays fixed at 0.0 while confidence is derived from payload validity and modulation margin.
