Pentacorder + SDAM — Field Sensing, Tools & Acoustic Comms
Fifteen live hardware channels, an eleven-tool sensor-driven multitool, a vitals & environment reader, a navigation console and a real sound-wave data link — everything reading straight from this device's own motion, orientation, GNSS, battery, microphone and camera hardware. This turns an ordinary phone or tablet into the pocket field instrument the original Pentacorder concept pointed at: real sensors, real math, nothing simulated. (The browser companion to the native SDAM / Pentacorder Android apps.)

SDAM — Software-Defined Acoustic Modem

Real sound-wave data link

IDLE
This encoder genuinely plays audio through the device speaker and genuinely listens through the microphone — it is a real (if simple) single-tone-per-nibble FSK scheme over the Web Audio API, not a math illustration. It is far simpler than the full OFDM link in the native 12-AZ-IP/14-sdam product: treat it as a working proof of an air-gapped acoustic channel, tested loopback on one device or between two devices placed near each other.
Base frequency
1760 Hz (A6) · 125 Hz/step, 16 tones
Symbol duration
120 ms/nibble + 30 ms gap
Encoding
UTF-8 byte → 2 nibbles → 2 tones, start/stop sentinel tones
Decode method
Live FFT (AnalyserNode) peak-bin matching, 48000 Hz
