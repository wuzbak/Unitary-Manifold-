---

Every Pillar in the Unitary Manifold has a structural mood. Some converge. Some close under pressure. Some stay permanently open. Some are beautiful because they work. Some are beautiful because they don't — and say so.

Rules remain strict:
- **One match per Pillar.** The most structurally resonant, not the most famous.
- **Structural precision over emotional analogy.** The music must mirror the *mathematics*.
- **No title-matching.** "DNA" for genetics, "Space Oddity" for astronomy, "Atom Heart Mother" for atomic structure — these are associations, not resonances. They were excluded.
- Works preferred over movements when the whole piece embodies the structure.
- Vocal works cited when text and structure genuinely coincide — when the words ARE the mathematics.

**What changed from v1:** The classical canon dominated v1. Bach appeared six times, Beethoven seven. Both composers belong here. But the framework spans 208 pillars and 5 decades of mathematics; its music guide should span more than two centuries of Western art music. Folk, jazz, rock, electronic, and experimental music contain structures just as precise as any fugue. Version 2 finds them.
