## The AxiomZero Hypothesis

**Fast, inexpensive, humane, and excellent are not competing goals. They are, properly understood, the same goal.**

This is not utopian. It is engineering. The hypothesis rests on three observations:
1. Every production system has a sustainable operating rate. Push past it and it degrades.
2. Preparation is the cheapest resource a production has.
3. The last 4 hours of a 14-hour day produce the worst footage, the most accidents, and the highest turnover.

## The 10-Hour Day Manifesto

The core practical claim: a 10-hour production day produces better work than a 14-hour day, at lower total cost, with lower turnover and higher quality. The last 4 hours of a 14-hour day produce:
- 20-30% higher error rate (cognitive performance drops after 10 hours)
- 3-4x higher incident rate
- The worst footage of the day (often requiring expensive reshoots)
- Crew burnout that drives experienced professionals out of the industry

The 10-hour day is not about making production easier. It is about making production smarter.
