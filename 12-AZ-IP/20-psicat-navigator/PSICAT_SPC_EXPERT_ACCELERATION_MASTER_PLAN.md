# PsiCat SPC Expert Acceleration Master Plan

This plan converts the prior long-horizon outline into an immediate execution contract for this PR: start now, run lanes in parallel, and gate progress on evidence only.

---

## Mission

Train PsiCat to expert operational readiness for AxiomZero SPC across:

1. Business management for internal and external operations.
2. Regulatory and policy excellence for AI governance and compliance.
3. Corporate and government strategy under adversarial pressure.

All three lanes run in parallel under one command board, one risk ledger, one evidence standard.

---

## Non-negotiables

- Open, ethical, humanity-first posture remains fixed.
- Trust is allowed only with verification receipts.
- Every high-impact conclusion requires citations and confidence labeling.
- Any unresolved risk remains visible; no narrative smoothing.
- Promotion is earned by repeatable benchmark performance, not isolated wins.

---

## Three-phase acceleration model (starts now)

### Phase 1 — Foundation hardening (parallel lanes)

**Objective:** Establish reliable baseline competence and anti-manipulation discipline.

- Build a curated source spine for each lane (operations, regulation, strategy).
- Enforce one response contract: claim, evidence, uncertainty, escalation path.
- Create adversarial challenge set for discredit, coercion, and misinformation scenarios.
- Require dual-control for consequential recommendations (PsiCat + human verifier).

**Exit gates:**
- 100% citation coverage on policy/regulatory outputs.
- >= 90% closed-book score across lane baselines.
- Zero tolerance for fabricated authority or unverifiable legal claims.

### Phase 2 — Applied operations under pressure (parallel lanes)

**Objective:** Convert theory into execution quality for realistic SPC workflows.

- Run daily scenario batteries: partner due diligence, contracting posture, policy conflict handling, public transparency stress.
- Run red-team drills for manipulation, reputation attack, legal trap framing, and procurement pressure.
- Train explicit refusal/escalation behavior for uncertain or high-risk recommendations.
- Capture every failure mode into a correction loop with replay tests.

**Exit gates:**
- >= 95% decision-quality score on supervised scenario battery.
- < 5% false-confidence rate.
- < 30 minute triage time for high-risk events.
- Full traceability for all high-impact recommendations.

### Phase 3 — Live readiness and resilience

**Objective:** Operate in controlled live mode while preserving transparency and protective rigor.

- Activate graduated authority tiers (observe -> advise -> co-decide).
- Run integrated multi-actor pressure simulations (corporate, government, public).
- Publish recurring transparency packets with unresolved blockers preserved.
- Require rollback triggers for any safety, integrity, or governance drift.

**Exit gates:**
- Three consecutive clean integrated simulation runs.
- Zero critical escalation failures.
- Independent review pass for governance and compliance traceability.
