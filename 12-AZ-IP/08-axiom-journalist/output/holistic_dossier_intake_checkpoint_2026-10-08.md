## Purpose and scope

This checkpoint preserves the successive investigator-submitted batches for the proposed holistic dossier on surveillance infrastructure, corporate concentration, public procurement, finance, and institutional accountability. It is a continuity record—not a completed investigation, legal brief, AXIOM database import, or publication draft. The submitted narrative contains many specific and testable leads, but the assertions and citations supplied in chat have not themselves been authenticated.

The working standard is the AXIOM OSINT Triangulation Chain: **identity → primary legal/regulatory record → independent corroboration**. An edge between two entities is admitted only when an identifier or a document connects them. Shared sector, nationality, military service, geography, technical vocabulary, timing, or political association alone does not establish a relationship, transfer, access path, coordination, intent, or wrongdoing.

### Evidence labels

- **CONFIRMED** — a court-adjudicated fact or explicit government admission, accurately limited to the ruling or admission.
- **CORROBORATED** — multiple genuinely independent primary records support the same material claim.
- **ALLEGED** — a credible attributable source supports the claim, but independent corroboration or primary records are absent.
- **UNVERIFIED** — the claim is supplied as a lead, cannot yet be tied to an adequate source, or has not been tested.

These labels describe evidence status, not moral importance or the likelihood that an accusation is true. A source-quality or progress score is not a probability of guilt.

### Key inference boundaries

1. **A subprocessor listing is not proof that every listed vendor receives every user payload.** Determine which data types each service handles and under what configuration.
2. **Encryption at rest and in transit is not end-to-end confidentiality from the service operator.** Determine where TLS terminates, whether payloads are decrypted for application/model processing, who controls keys, and what logs persist. Do not imply that an inference vendor can or cannot read a request without that evidence.
3. **A vendor’s jurisdiction, an employee’s service history, or a founder’s biography does not establish government access or tasking.** A government-access claim needs an identified legal or technical pathway and evidence that it was used or is available under the stated circumstances.
4. **Technical capability is not an observed vulnerability.** “Unauthenticated API,” cross-tenant leakage, hidden telemetry, or access-control bypass requires a scoped, authorized reproduction, credible disclosure, or independent audit. No such test has been performed for this intake.
5. **A military system and a commercial product do not share lineage merely because they use similar methods or engineers.** Prove the transfer with dated technical artifacts, patents, contracts, project records, or first-hand corroborated testimony.
6. **The prior Azure reporting does not establish an AWS migration.** Microsoft’s public statement, journalistic reporting, and any AWS hosting claim must remain separate source-linked claims.
7. **Contract clauses are not automatically unlawful.** Quote the full clause and neighboring provisions, identify the governing law, and obtain qualified legal review before characterizing enforceability or statutory violations.
