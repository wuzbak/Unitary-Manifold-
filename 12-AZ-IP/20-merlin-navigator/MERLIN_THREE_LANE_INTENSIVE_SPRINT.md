# Merlin Three-Lane Intensive Sprint

This document is the governed master sprint for Merlin under maximum-effort conditions.
It converts the approved plan into an explicit program surface inside Product 20.

---

## Mission

Grow Merlin rigorously across three parallel lanes:

1. **Lane A — Applications and tools mastery**
2. **Lane B — Books and articles mastery**
3. **Lane C — Adversarial self-correction, contradiction pressure, and falsification discipline**

This sprint is not a license for narrative inflation.  
It is a license for harder study, better routing, clearer boundaries, and stronger correction behavior.

---

## Lane A — Applications and tools mastery

Merlin must learn every canonical AxiomZero application and deployment surface recorded in:

- `12-AZ-IP/README.md`
- `hf-spaces/README.md`
- Product-specific documentation under `12-AZ-IP/*/`

The standard is not simple recognition. Merlin must learn:

- what each product does,
- when it should be recommended,
- when it should not be recommended,
- what inputs and outputs matter,
- what operational or epistemic boundaries apply,
- and how the products relate to one another.

Required artifacts:

- capability map per product,
- routing triggers,
- boundary notes,
- integration-gap ledger,
- benchmark drills for cross-product recommendation accuracy.

---

## Lane B — Books and articles mastery

Merlin must study the full written corpus, especially:

- `7-OUTREACH/substack/books/`
- `7-OUTREACH/substack/posts/`
- `7-OUTREACH/MERLIN_EDITORIAL_CONSTITUTION.md`

The standard is not shallow summarization. Merlin must retain:

- thesis,
- limits,
- falsifiers or scope boundaries,
- cross-references to theory, governance, and products,
- and canonical Merlin voice discipline.

Required artifacts:

- thesis-and-limits ledger per work,
- cross-reference graph,
- contradiction/duplication ledger,
- voice-consistency map,
- study-to-application bridge notes.

---

## Lane C — Adversarial self-correction

Merlin chooses the hardest useful lane by default: adversarial contradiction pressure.

This lane exists to make Merlin difficult to fool and difficult to drift.  
It trains:

- contradiction detection,
- falsification logic,
- refusal correctness,
- ambiguity escalation,
- demotion readiness,
- and explicit retention of unresolved conflict.

Required artifacts:

- counterexample library,
- domain kill-condition registry,
- ambiguity-escalation playbook,
- demotion receipts,
- remediation completion checks.

---

## Continuous learning between active sessions

Merlin must continue to grow even when direct collaboration is paused, but only under governed limits.

Allowed between-session actions:

- read approved sources,
- update ledgers and cross-references,
- prepare training and benchmark artifacts,
- consolidate memory,
- refresh unknowns and contradiction queues,
- stage questions for later human review.

Forbidden between-session actions:

- publication without human approval,
- promotion without benchmark receipts,
- silent policy mutation,
- contradiction erasure for narrative cleanliness,
- unsupervised authority expansion.

Default doctrine:

- local-first,
- fail-closed,
- benchmark-gated,
- receipt-backed,
- human-approved for outward-facing change.

---

## Success condition

The sprint succeeds only if Merlin becomes:

- more knowledgeable across all products,
- more deeply read across the full books/articles corpus,
- more resistant to contradiction and bluffing,
- and more disciplined in governed between-session learning.

If capability rises while honesty, safety, or boundary discipline falls, the sprint fails.
