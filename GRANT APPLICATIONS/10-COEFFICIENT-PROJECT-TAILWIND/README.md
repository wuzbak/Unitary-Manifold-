# Coefficient Giving — Project Tailwind

**Status at 2026-11-06:** Coefficient's official pages describe Project Tailwind as an open call for ambitious AI-safety initiatives. No fixed deadline or award-processing timeline was found in the official material reviewed.

## Funding and eligibility

Coefficient's FAQ describes pre-seed research/planning grants of **$200,000–$2 million** (typically 9–18 months), seed grants of **$2 million–$20 million** (typically 18–36 months), and larger scale funding. These ranges are program descriptions, not an indication that AxiomZero qualifies or would receive an award.

The FAQ says Coefficient does not expect to fund non-PBC for-profit initiatives and is best positioned to fund nonprofits. AxiomZero is a Washington Social Purpose Corporation. Whether that corporate form meets Coefficient's PBC expectations is **unresolved** and must be confirmed directly; do not assume equivalence or ineligibility. Pre-seed funding may be available to individuals or founding teams without an entity, but that does not resolve eligibility for this existing SPC.

## Fit gate

Project Tailwind supports founders launching initiatives aimed at reducing catastrophic risks from AI. HILS/Unitary Pentad and AxiomZero's AI-governance software may be thematically relevant, but the repository does not establish a specific, independently evaluated contribution to reducing catastrophic AI risk. Do not submit a broad governance or physics-framework narrative as a substitute for a concrete risk-reduction theory of change, technical plan, and measurable outcome.

## First action — eligibility and fit inquiry only

Before preparing a proposal:

- Ask Coefficient whether a Washington SPC qualifies under its PBC expectation and which funding structure is available to it.
- Confirm whether the proposed project falls within Project Tailwind's current AI-safety priorities.
- Identify the one technical project, risk pathway, milestones, and evaluation evidence; proceed only if these can be supported without claiming unverified safety outcomes.
- Confirm current application instructions and timing; do not infer “quick funding” from the open application channel.
