# NASA SBIR Phase I

**Status at 2026-11-06:** NASA's 2026 Phase I solicitation closed May 21, 2026. NASA's program began using a Broad Agency Announcement with topic appendices; no next Phase I topic or deadline is assumed here.

## Program facts and fit

NASA lists up to **$225,000** and a six-month Phase I period. The program supports small-business technology aligned with NASA missions and commercial potential. AxiomZero's general physics framework is not enough: a proposal must answer a specific NASA topic and demonstrate mission relevance.

## Conditional concept

Monitor future appendices for topics in data systems, scientific software, navigation/monitoring, or AI assurance only if exact topic text fits an existing product and available technical capacity. Frame the work as a testable technology development effort with a NASA-relevant baseline and commercial customers beyond NASA. Do not reuse the NOAA abstract without changing the technical objectives and topic crosswalk.

## Before a future application

- Read current BAA and Appendix topic text and verify business eligibility.
- Confirm SAM.gov/UEI, NASA registration, PI role, ownership, work-share, and required forms.
- Obtain a mission-domain reviewer and identify customer discovery evidence.
- Create topic-specific milestones, budget, and commercialization plan.
