## Applicant positioning

Lead with **the research question, transparent methods, falsifiable or measurable outcomes, and the bounded application being developed**—not claims of customer validation or broad claims about an entire repository. The Unitary Manifold is a 5D Kaluza–Klein research framework; its scientific claims remain subject to documented open problems, tensions, peer review, and external confirmation (`README.md`, `STATUS.md`, `FALLIBILITY.md`). The Unitary Pentad/HILS governance work is an independent governance framework and must not be presented as validated by the physics. Strongest repository-listed application evidence includes:

- **EIGE Governance Engine:** listed at TRL 7 with 449 tests.
- **AxiomZero SGE:** listed at TRL 7 with 229 tests.
- **UM Geophysical Monitor:** listed at TRL 5 with 121 tests and live data-source integrations.
- **PsiCat Navigator:** listed at TRL 4 with 149 tests.
- **SDAM:** the detailed product specification calls this the Software-Defined Acoustic Modem, an Android acoustic-communications prototype. The consolidated product catalog instead calls it “Spatial Decision Awareness Module.” Resolve this naming and description conflict before any external submission.
- **Quantum lane:** includes a braided-qubit ansatz studio listed at TRL 2; it is early-stage and is not evidence of quantum hardware advantage.

The AZ-SGE catalog description advertises anti-malware, zero-day detection, IDS, and firewall capabilities; its detailed product README instead describes local analysis and explicitly says host telemetry and enforcement are not implemented. Use the detailed README's narrower scope in any application, and resolve this catalog/specification discrepancy before external submission. Product-catalog maturity levels and test counts are company-reported, not independently assessed.

The detailed SDAM specification describes an Android acoustic-communications prototype with encrypted/authenticated text handling. It also documents that cross-device key provisioning, real-device acoustic performance, and native build verification remain incomplete. The portfolio will only consider a consent-based, authorized, non-sensitive research evaluation; it will not frame SDAM as covert communication, an air-gap bypass, or a method for moving protected data. The governance work should be described accurately: HILS/Unitary Pentad is the governance framework; EIGE is election-integrity/governance software; AZ-SGE is local security analysis (not host enforcement). Do not collapse these into one generic product. Repository-listed maturity/test counts are not independent validation, customer adoption, security certification, or proof of scientific correctness. The work is explicitly described by the company as research-stage and not production-ready (`docs/policy/AXIOMZERO_SPC.md`). Do not pitch theoretical claims as validated products or describe tests as proof of the theory.

### Research-first proposal lanes

- **Foundational theory:** propose one bounded derivation, mathematical question, or reproducibility study with assumptions, methods, falsifiers, and open limitations visible. The 5D framework is not externally confirmed; a theory proposal must not treat internal tests as empirical validation.
- **Applications research:** select one research-stage tool and one measurable question. SDAM’s detailed specification is an unusually distinctive research direction, but its product name conflicts with the portfolio catalog and its device/key-validation gaps must be resolved. Any proposed evaluation must use consenting endpoints, synthetic or non-sensitive data, and an authorized lab setting.
- **Governance research:** treat HILS/Unitary Pentad as a governance framework independent of the physics; identify a bounded governance claim and evaluation method, and do not claim institutional adoption or impact without evidence.

Do not combine all three lanes into one proposal. Prefer the one whose published funding notice, methods, independent reviewer, and allowable applicant type best match the specific work.

### Novelty and credibility standard

“Novel” must mean a specific, supportable difference from prior art—not “first,” “unique,” or “breakthrough” by assertion. For SDAM, the defensible current distinction is a research-stage Android integration of acoustic audio I/O with encrypted/authenticated text handling and a visible status path. The specification identifies ggwave and standard cryptographic primitives as dependencies; it does not establish a new modulation method, secure peer provisioning, dependable device-to-device performance, acoustic containment, RF isolation, or field readiness. Any application must compare the proposed research question with existing acoustic-modem and offline-communications work, cite the comparison, and focus on measurable validation gaps.

For AI governance, explain the actual HILS/Unitary Pentad principles and artifacts, then identify the specific system being evaluated. The framework is independent of the physics; repository tests demonstrate software behavior only, not governance impact or external adoption. For theoretical physics, distinguish derived, fitted, adjacent, and open claims using the repository's epistemic ledgers. This explicit boundary is part of AxiomZero's credibility, not something to soften for a funder.

## Hard gates and application hygiene

- Never submit without the current official solicitation and a completed eligibility checklist.
- Do not assume a Washington SPC automatically satisfies SBIR ownership/control, small-business, PI, or affiliate rules.
- Register early where required: SAM.gov/UEI, agency systems, and authorized organizational representative accounts. Do not share passwords or tokens with helpers.
- Budgets in these briefs are **not estimates**. Prepare a costed budget only after scope, project duration, salary, partner/subaward, indirect-cost, and matching rules are known.
- Do not claim a customer, partnership, user count, deployment, revenue, external contribution, certification, or physical result without records and permission.
- Keep each application scoped to one product/project. Never double-charge the same effort across awards.
- Record decisions, submissions, and funder correspondence in the relevant opportunity folder. Do not put personal identifiers, tax records, bank details, credentials, or confidential application materials in this public repository.
