3. **NSF Project Pitch:** choose one commercializable product and customer problem; collect customer discovery evidence before writing the pitch. The Nov. 4, 2026 full-proposal date has passed; use the next eligible deadline only after NSF invitation.
4. **DOE topic screen:** map the published FY26 Fall topic text line-by-line to a technically credible energy or cybersecurity project. Stop if fit requires changing the project's actual purpose.
5. **Small applications:** prepare a security-specific GitHub Fund request and a separate, quantum-specific Unitary Foundation microgrant proposal only after verifying current forms, terms, and applicant/payee requirements.
6. **Build the evidence base:** identify two external pilot users, one independent scientific/software reviewer, and a grant accountant/administrator. Do not claim these people or commitments until secured.

- **Quantum lane:** includes a braided-qubit ansatz studio listed at TRL 2; it is early-stage and is not evidence of quantum hardware advantage.

These are repository-reported maturity/test counts, not independent validation, customer adoption, security certification, or proof of scientific correctness. The work is explicitly described by the company as research-stage and not production-ready (`docs/policy/AXIOMZERO_SPC.md`). The physics framework is not externally confirmed; do not pitch theoretical claims as validated products or describe tests as proof of the theory.

## Hard gates and application hygiene

- Never submit without the current official solicitation and a completed eligibility checklist.
- Do not assume a Washington SPC automatically satisfies SBIR ownership/control, small-business, PI, or affiliate rules.
- Register early where required: SAM.gov/UEI, agency systems, and authorized organizational representative accounts. Do not share passwords or tokens with helpers.
- Budgets in these briefs are **not estimates**. Prepare a costed budget only after scope, project duration, salary, partner/subaward, indirect-cost, and matching rules are known.
- Do not claim a customer, partnership, user count, deployment, revenue, external contribution, certification, or physical result without records and permission.
- Keep each application scoped to one product/project. Never double-charge the same effort across awards.
- Record decisions, submissions, and funder correspondence in the relevant opportunity folder. Do not put personal identifiers, tax records, bank details, credentials, or confidential application materials in this public repository.
