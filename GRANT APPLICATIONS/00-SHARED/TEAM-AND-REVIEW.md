# Working Team and Review Protocol

This is the operating model for developing the 12 grant prospects. AxiomZero is a research-and-development organization and has no customers as of 2026-11-06; the human corporate representative has final authority. AI helpers support research and drafting but do not make certifications, commitments, eligibility decisions, or submissions. Grant writing must not imply customers, pilots, or commercial validation that do not exist.

Task-scoped AI helpers have supported funding discovery, repository evidence review, official-source auditing, SDAM funding research, and independent grant-writing critique. They are not employees, retained staff, or human collaborators. No customers or outside collaborators are represented as committed.

## Required review gates

1. **Notice gate:** retrieve and archive the official NOFO/solicitation and amendments; record access date, deadline/time zone, award instrument, match, and registrations.
2. **Eligibility gate:** complete the program-specific checklist from the official notice. The PI confirms it in writing; unknown facts mean HOLD.
3. **Evidence gate:** every factual claim maps to a source in `EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md` or a dated external citation. Distinguish company statements, repo test results, and independent validation.
4. **Technical red-team:** an independent human reviewer checks novelty, hypothesis, methods, baselines, failure criteria, data rights, security, and ability to finish on time.
5. **Budget gate:** accountant/grants administrator validates line items, rates, subawards, cost share, taxes, and no duplicate effort.
6. **Final certification:** PI reads the complete submission, confirms every certification, submits through the authorized account, and retains the confirmation receipt in a non-public records system.

## Confidentiality and integrity

Do not place credentials, bank/tax data, signatures, nonpublic financials, personal identifiers, or confidential partner documents in this repository. Do not permit a helper to submit, sign, contact a funder as the applicant, or create a funding commitment without explicit human direction. Follow each funder's rules for AI assistance and disclose AI-generated content when required.
