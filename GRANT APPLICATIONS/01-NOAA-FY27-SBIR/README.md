# NOAA FY27 SBIR — Phase I

**Status at 2026-11-06:** upcoming notice; NOAA says FY27 cycle begins in Fall 2026 and targets releasing the NOFO Nov. 10–30, 2026. **Do not rely on an aggregator's date or amount until the notice is published.**

## Why consider it

UM Geophysical Monitor is listed in the product catalog as a TRL-5 research-stage prototype with 121 repository-reported tests and USGS/NASA EONET feeds. It is not a NOAA product, operational forecast, or validated warning system. SDAM is a separate research-stage acoustic-communications project and currently has no established NOAA topic match. NOAA's past topics have included weather, water, climate, coastal resilience, hazards, AI, and data/cloud computing; the FY27 notice must establish an exact match before a tool is proposed. AxiomZero has no customers and will not claim customer validation.

## Proposed project angle — conditional

**Working title:** *Reproducible Evaluation of [Specific FY27 NOAA Topic] Data/Decision-Support Method.*

Select one FY27 topic only after release. A possible research question is whether a bounded, provenance-preserving workflow can improve a topic-defined monitoring or decision-support task when compared with an explicit baseline. The proposal must name an actual NOAA-relevant task, legal data sources, evaluation unit, error costs, and independent reviewer. Keep repository physics-overlay outputs separate from observational measurements unless the solicitation explicitly supports testing them; do not present them as NOAA-validated signals. Do not describe the current prototype as an operational warning or forecasting system.

## Fit and gates

- NOAA's applicant page describes eligibility for American-owned, independently operated, for-profit U.S. small businesses with fewer than 500 employees including affiliates; the PI must be employed at least 51% by the firm, and U.S. work-share rules apply. Verify the FY27 notice.
- NOAA currently lists Phase I awards up to $190,000 for up to 6 months and Phase II up to $690,000 for up to 24 months; these are current-program reference figures, **not a promise for FY27**.
- Proceed only if a published FY27 topic directly matches an actual Geo Monitor capability and there is a commercialization path beyond the agency.
- **No-go rule:** if the published FY27 notice has no topic that directly matches an existing capability and research question, record NO-GO. Do not select the nearest topic or reshape Geo Monitor to claim a capability it does not have.
- A NOAA-aligned pilot/data expert would materially strengthen the plan; do not name a partner until confirmed.

## Draft abstract

AxiomZero Technologies & Consulting, SPC is a Washington research-and-development company with no customers as of November 6, 2026. We propose to study [specific FY27 NOAA topic and user task] using a bounded, research-stage software method and authorized data. We will define representative cases and a baseline with a domain reviewer, record data provenance and uncertainty, and compare results on pre-specified measures of [task-specific performance], [error cost], and [reproducibility]. The evaluation will include negative cases and failure analysis. No customer adoption, NOAA endorsement, operational forecast skill, or warning capability is claimed. Phase I will determine whether the approach is technically feasible and has a justified path to further development.

## Work plan (six months; revise to the NOFO)

1. Confirm exact topic, prospective user task, data rights, success thresholds, and baseline.
2. Establish test fixtures and a reproducible evaluation protocol.
3. Implement only the topic-relevant prototype capability.
4. Evaluate on held-out scenarios and document failures.
5. Conduct prospective-user discovery and produce a Phase II/commercialization decision; do not represent interviewees as customers.
