# Alfred P. Sloan Foundation — Open Source in Science

**Status:** the program accepts letters of inquiry through a program-specific process and the foundation makes grants year-round. The current award ceiling and eligibility of a for-profit Washington SPC were not established in this research. Treat this as an **eligibility inquiry**, not an application-ready prospect.

## Program alignment

The program seeks tools, norms, and institutions that support distributed development, adoption, and maintenance of discovery-enabling software, hardware, and models. It focuses on the research-software ecosystem rather than simply funding a single open-source codebase. AxiomZero's open-science work may be relevant only if framed as an institutional/economic model with external collaborators and a field-wide benefit.

## Draft inquiry (not for sending until authorized)

> AxiomZero Technologies & Consulting, SPC is a Washington social-purpose corporation developing open-science software and governance tools. We are assessing whether a proposed collaboration to [specific ecosystem objective] fits the Foundation's Open Source in Science program. The project would [brief activities] with [confirmed independent partners] and make [outputs] available under [verified licenses]. Could you advise whether an SPC may apply directly, whether a nonprofit/university lead or fiscal sponsor is required, and whether the proposed project is within the program's current priorities?

## Gates

- Ask the program director about SPC eligibility, lead-applicant status, and acceptable fiscal sponsors.
- Review recent awards for scope, award range, and recipient type.
- Develop a multi-organization ecosystem proposal only if a real partner network and maintenance plan exist.
- Follow Sloan's LOI instructions and timing; do not assume “rolling” means immediate funding.
