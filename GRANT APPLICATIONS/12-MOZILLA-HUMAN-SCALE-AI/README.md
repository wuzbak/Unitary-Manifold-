# Mozilla Foundation — Human-Scale AI Incubator

**Status at 2026-11-06:** the current call closes **November 20, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Standard Time**. Mozilla offers up to **$500,000 per selected project over three years**; this is a maximum, not expected funding. The FAQ says applicants would receive a status update by late February 2027; that is not an award or payment date.

## Fit and hard gates

Mozilla seeks technical projects creating fit-for-purpose AI alternatives and emphasizes product-community fit. Its FAQ says an applicant needs working technology that people can use, and any selected team must be able to receive funds **from** a U.S.-based 501(c)(3). For-profit applicants must clearly articulate the charitable nature of the work.

This wording describes the grantmaker, not a requirement that the applicant be a nonprofit or have a fiscal sponsor. The earlier version of this brief incorrectly turned "from" into a 501(c)(3) funding-recipient requirement. No mandatory fiscal-sponsor requirement is established by the quoted FAQ; determine the SPC's actual eligibility from the full eligibility guidelines and funder confirmation.

AxiomZero has no customers, as confirmed by the founder. That fact alone does not establish whether the project fits: the repository does not establish the external user/community evidence or the SPC's eligibility under the complete guidelines. These are unknowns to verify, not assumptions. Do not claim a pilot, deployment, or product-community fit without records.

## Project selection hold

No application concept is approved in this brief. Before drafting, identify a specific working technical product and confirm it actually fits the call's human-scale AI goals, can be evaluated with its intended community, and is eligible for support. A governance framework alone or a general theoretical-physics proposal is not sufficient. If the technical fit, community evidence, or applicant eligibility cannot be established before the deadline, record NO-GO for this cycle.

## Immediate checklist

- Confirm the call remains open and read the complete official call and FAQ.
- Read the full eligibility guidelines and confirm whether the SPC and its proposed charitable project qualify to receive an award; do not assume a fiscal sponsor is mandatory.
- Inventory existing user/community evidence for the selected working technology; do not infer users from repository test counts.
- Confirm AI-assisted-drafting disclosure, IP, budget, reporting, and payment terms in the application materials.
- Do not submit unless the authorized human representative approves the project, eligibility route, evidence, and all certifications.
