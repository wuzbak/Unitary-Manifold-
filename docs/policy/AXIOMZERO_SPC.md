## 5 · Scope of Operations

AxiomZero Technologies & Consulting, SPC is established to develop and deploy
AI governance systems, open-science frameworks, and consulting services grounded in
the Unitary Manifold framework.  Key activities include:

- **Responsible AI Governance** — Design and deployment of Human-in-the-Loop Systems
  (HILS), the Unitary Pentad governance framework, and structured AI accountability layers.
- **Open-Science Infrastructure** — Maintenance and publication of the Unitary Manifold
  research framework as an irrevocably open, public-domain physics theory with a full
  open-source implementation under AGPL-3.0.
- **Scientific Consulting** — Expert advisory services applying 5D Kaluza-Klein geometry,
  the Walker-Pearson field equations, and adjacent research tracks to client domains.
- **Domain Calibration** — The primary commercial value-add: tuning the framework's
  parameters for a client's specific organisational, regulatory, or infrastructure context.
- **Environmental & Public-Good Applications** — Adjacent research tracks and consulting
  work directed at climate, ecology, marine science, and governance challenges.
- **Commercial License Exceptions (CLEs)** — For enterprises requiring proprietary
  deployment of the AGPL-covered implementation without source-disclosure obligations.
  See [`COMMERCIAL_TERMS.md`](COMMERCIAL_TERMS.md) § 4-A.
