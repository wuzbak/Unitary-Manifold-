### Where wₐ = 0 Comes From

In the Unitary Manifold, dark energy is the vacuum energy of the radion field φ — the size modulus of the fifth dimension. The radion is stabilized by the FTUM (Fixed-Point Unitary Map) contraction, which drives φ to a self-consistent vacuum φ₀ through an iterative attractor.

At the fixed point:
- The radion potential V(φ) = V₀ is flat (stabilized)
- The equation of state of the radion vacuum: w = pᵩ/ρᵩ = (T − V)/(T + V)
- At the fixed point, kinetic energy T → 0 (φ is stabilized, not rolling)
- Therefore: w = (0 − V₀)/(0 + V₀) = −1 exactly, with wₐ = 0

A time-varying wₐ ≠ 0 would require the radion to be **rolling** rather than stabilized. In the RS1 geometry with the FTUM fixed point, this would require either:
- Fine-tuning the initial conditions so the radion is near but not at the fixed point, requiring a new free parameter (the displacement), or
- An unstabilized radion, which would destabilize the hierarchy and break the SM parameter derivations

Either option destroys the framework's internal consistency. wₐ ≠ 0 is not a small correction — it would require structural revision of the stabilization mechanism.

**This is why wₐ = 0 is certified as ARCHITECTURE_LIMIT (Pillar 301):** no modification within the current 5D-EFT can produce wₐ ≈ −0.55 (the DESI DR2 central value) without requiring fine-tuning of order ε_GW ~ 10⁻⁸⁸ (the Goldberger-Wise stabilization parameter).
