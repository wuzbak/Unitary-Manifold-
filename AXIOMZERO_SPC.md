## Social Purpose Statement

AxiomZero Technologies & Consulting, SPC is incorporated as a Social Purpose Corporation
under Washington State law (RCW 23B.25). The stated social purpose is:

> To advance open, falsifiable, publicly verifiable science through human-AI collaboration;
> to make the Unitary Manifold physics framework available to humanity under a Defensive
> Public Commons License; and to develop AI-augmented governance, physics, and technology
> tools that prioritize transparency, epistemic honesty, and public benefit over private
> extraction.
