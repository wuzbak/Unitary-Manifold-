# P1133 Research Brief — Superfluid Connections and Their Limits

Date: 2026-11-05

## Research question

Can established superfluid physics provide a rigorous comparison or test for
Unitary Manifold ideas, and which of the apparent links are only analogies?

## Findings

The P1133 implementation evaluates the leading-order Bogoliubov spectrum for a
uniform, zero-temperature, dilute, repulsively interacting three-dimensional
Bose gas. With particle mass \(m\), number density \(n\), wave number \(k\),
and s-wave scattering length \(a_s\), it uses

\[
g = 4\pi\hbar^2 a_s/m,\quad
\epsilon_k = \hbar^2 k^2/(2m),\quad
E_k = \sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2gn)},\quad
c_s = \sqrt{gn/m}.
\]

This is an established reference calculation under its stated assumptions.
It is not a calculation of the repository's 5D fields or an inferred
superfluid phase of the Unitary Manifold.

Son's relativistic superfluid effective theory is a separate framework: its
low-energy Goldstone action is organized by the equation of state. To use it
as a genuine UM connection would require identifying the relevant symmetry,
order parameter, and UM-derived equation of state, then carrying out the
matching. Those ingredients have not been supplied.

Visser's acoustic metric shows that perturbations in barotropic, inviscid,
irrotational fluid flow can be written as a scalar wave equation on an
effective Lorentzian geometry. This is a legitimate geometric analogy for
wave propagation. It does not derive Einstein dynamics or show that
spacetime itself is a fluid. Laboratory analogue-horizon experiments test
wave behavior in a medium, not astrophysical black-hole radiation.

Superfluid dark matter (SFDM) is a model-dependent astrophysical proposal.
Berezhiani and Khoury proposed that dark matter could form superfluid phases
in galactic environments, with phonon-mediated forces producing
MOND-like phenomenology, while clusters may occupy a mixed phase. Its
parameter choices, self-interaction and merger constraints, and questions
such as core fragmentation remain issues for that model. These hypotheses
are neither implied by the Bogoliubov calculation nor evidence for UM.

## Conclusion and next checks

The supported connection is methodological: superfluid EFT and analogue
models supply explicit assumptions and equations against which an eventual
UM-derived order parameter, equation of state, or propagation law could be
compared. No such mapping or distinct UM prediction has been derived here.

Next checks are to establish the dilute-gas regime (including the gas
parameter), compare against independent spectra, and evaluate SFDM
constraints only after specifying a particular astrophysical model.
