# S04E009 — Sprint CA: CMB Amplitude Integrity Without Hidden Calibration

**Historical note:** this article preserves Sprint CA's CMB-lane framing while aligning it to the current evidentiary scope. The historical `[3.45, 4.85]` deficit interval and associated budget shift are carried forward as inherited inputs, not as a new independently recalculated contraction. The present repository position is stricter: the pivot normalization uses an observational `A_s` target, and the terminal EFT routes do not yet derive the missing amplitude mechanism.

The reason this lane matters is simple. A framework can often make itself look better by blurring the difference between calibration and prediction. Sprint CA was valuable when it resisted that temptation, and it remains valuable now for the same reason.

## What changed

- The CMB lane preserved the no-fit rule: no external `A_s` target could be smuggled in and then re-described as a first-principles prediction.
- The inherited post-campaign deficit envelope remained explicit:
  - **lower: 3.45**
  - **upper: 4.85**
- The lane kept the two named missing objects visible in the evidence ledger:
  - **nonperturbative amplitude-generation mechanism**
  - **global UV completion of transfer normalization**
- The repository linked this sprint packet to the broader CMB boundary audit, which states plainly that calibration is not prediction.

What survived the later reassessment is the integrity rule, not a claim of newly earned contraction.

## What did not change

- `closure_earned` stayed **False**.
- `CMB_AMP_CONFIRMED_IRREDUCIBLE` stayed open as a lane identifier.
- No independently derived primordial amplitude was produced.
- No transfer-function completion removed the ×4–7 acoustic-peak deficit burden.

That is the honest center of the story. Sprint CA kept the problem named and prevented a cosmetic rescue, but it did not solve the problem.

## Falsification implications

The decisive scientific question remains unchanged: can the framework produce the amplitude/transfer story without leaning on an observational target as an input? If not, this lane remains open.

There is also a methodological falsifier. If later prose quietly treats a calibration identity as a prediction, the integrity rule built into this lane has failed even before new sky data arrive. That is why the article is worth expanding instead of leaving as a shell: the boundary between “matched by input” and “derived from theory” is one of the most important honesty boundaries in the whole repository.

## Residual unknowns

- The first-principles amplitude-generation mechanism is still absent.
- The UV transfer-normalization completion is still absent.
- The inherited historical interval is useful for traceability but is not a fresh proof of narrowing.
- This remains one of the clearest places where the repository must prefer explicit incompleteness over impressive phrasing.
