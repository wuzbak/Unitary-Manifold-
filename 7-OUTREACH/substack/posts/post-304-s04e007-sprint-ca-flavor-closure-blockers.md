# S04E007 — Sprint CA: Flavor Closure Blockers, Ranked Without Spin

The claim in this post is narrow: Sprint CA improved the flavor-lane blocker map without earning a runtime closure flip. This claim is falsified if any lane is presented as closed without crossing its threshold, or if unresolved flavor objects are removed from the ledger by wording alone.

Flavor has been one of the most leverage-sensitive lanes in the branch because the same unresolved structures pressure multiple outcomes at once. Sprint CA did not solve that; it made the dependency structure explicit enough to prevent self-deception.

## What changed

- The flavor continuation path inherited the Sprint BY campaign and **enriched each blocker row** with:
  - excess over threshold,
  - closure ratio,
  - distance to threshold,
  - pressure ranking.
- Two priority signals were made explicit:
  - **closest lane to a runtime flip**, and
  - **hardest remaining lane** under current constraints.
- Dependency families were formalized so we can see when one unresolved object blocks several lanes simultaneously:
  - shared-root coupling,
  - phase-completion coupling,
  - species-resolution coupling.
- Sprint CA integrated this flavor ranking into a cross-lane deterministic decision matrix rather than leaving it as an isolated dashboard.

## What did not change

- No flavor runtime label flipped to a closed state.
- No new free parameter was introduced to force a pass.
- No blocker family was hidden to make progress appear larger than it is.
- The flavor-related open-lane architecture-limit labels remained explicit.

## Falsification implications

- The decisive falsifier here is methodological: if future updates claim flavor closure while unresolved shared objects still dominate pressure, this series becomes non-credible.
- External observational windows (DESI, LiteBIRD) are unchanged by this lane-specific progress; flavor tightening does not bypass those tests.
- The genuine gain is auditability: we now have a reproducible ordering of where effort should go next and why.

## Residual unknowns

- Shared flavor-root objects remain unresolved at closure grade.
- Global CKM phase completion beyond current EFT-cap framing remains open.
- Species-resolved radii/geometry locking still blocks full closure.
- The final proof burden is not “more work in general,” but specific unresolved objects with measurable threshold distance.
