## What improved

It also narrowed the next-step burden for Merlin and the human loop. The repository no longer has to juggle diffuse ambiguity here; it now has a specific foundation lane with explicit obligations.

---

## What remains open

These are not cosmetic gaps. They are the remaining load-bearing questions for this lane.

## Why this is still real progress

A lot of projects inflate by renaming unresolved work as momentum.
This sprint does the opposite: it contracts the fog.

A smaller blocker set gives better science and better engineering because it limits where confusion can hide. Fewer open claims, cleaner boundaries, tighter next actions.

That is what mature closeout language should do.

## Bottom line
