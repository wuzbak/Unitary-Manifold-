## Why non-approval language is essential

A system can be useful and still unfit for broad autonomous authority.
This post preserves that boundary intentionally.

Readiness here means “stronger constrained operation,” not “general approval.”

## Governance falsifier

A blocker must be able to stop promotion or it is not a blocker.
