## Editorial and evidentiary contract

This is a public-interest investigation, not a verdict. It follows the AXIOM Journalist case file at `/12-AZ-IP/08-axiom-journalist/output/unit8200_surveillance_investigation.json` and the two-volume dossier beside it. Those records separate what a company has publicly said, what journalists have reported, what remains a lead, and what the present evidence does not establish.

The same discipline governs every part. “Confirmed” is not a synonym for important. A high-stakes allegation remains an allegation until adequate evidence supports more. Corporate announcements establish what a company announced; they do not establish hidden purposes. A source’s nationality, military service, or professional network is not evidence of disloyalty. An unanswered question is not an answer.

**Publication posture:** research draft; human fact-checking, source retrieval, right of reply, technical review, and legal review remain outstanding. Nothing in this series should be quoted as an adjudicated finding or as proof of unlawful conduct.
