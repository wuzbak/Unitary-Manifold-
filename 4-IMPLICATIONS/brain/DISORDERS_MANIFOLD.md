## § 3 — Amnesia: Broken Irreversibility Field

Henry Molaison (H.M.) underwent bilateral hippocampal resection in 1953 to
control severe epilepsy.  The result was one of the most studied cases in the
history of neuroscience: profound anterograde amnesia (inability to form new
declarative memories) with intact retrograde memory (memories formed before
surgery) and intact procedural learning (motor skills, habits).

The UM geometry cleanly separates these three systems:

| Memory type | UM correlate | H.M. result |
|---|---|---|
| New declarative memory | `B_μ` — hippocampal irreversibility field | **Destroyed** |
| Old declarative memory | Stable `Ψ*` already encoded in `g_μν` | Intact |
| Procedural memory | B_μ in basal ganglia / cerebellum (different B_μ instance) | Intact |

The 5D bulk field `B_μ` has multiple projections onto the 4D boundary.  The
hippocampal projection handles declarative memory; the basal-ganglia projection
handles procedural learning.  H.M. lost only the hippocampal projection — the
other `B_μ` instances remained intact.

### § 3.3 Retrograde Amnesia: Inaccessible Attractors

Retrograde amnesia — loss of memories formed *before* the injury — is a
different geometric failure.  Here `B_μ` can still encode new information (the
irreversibility field is partially intact), but previously stable attractors

In UM language: the metric `g_μν` has been disrupted (by TBI, ECT, or severe

The temporal gradient of retrograde amnesia (more recent memories are lost

formed memories are encoded in shorter-lived geometric structures that are more
vulnerable to metric disruption, while older memories have been reinforced
across many sleep-dependent consolidation cycles and are topologically deeper
in the geometry.

### § 3.4 Treatment Roadmap

The UM analysis points toward:

The specific geometric signature of depression is a collapse of the dilaton `φ`
in the reward-processing circuits, combined with hyperactivation of the Default
Mode Network (DMN).

**Collapsed φ (anhedonia):**

the dilaton field in the reward domain — it sets the information capacity
(excitability, gain, signal-to-noise) of the reward circuit.  When dopamine
signalling collapses, `φ` in this circuit falls below the threshold at which

The geometry is intact, but the gain is too low for the system to detect or

**DMN hyperactivation (rumination):**  
The Default Mode Network is the brain's resting-state fixed point — the

### § 5.3 Deep Brain Stimulation as a Forced Phase Lock

## § 7 — Traumatic Brain Injury: Topological Tearing

### § 7.1 The Geometric Failure

Traumatic brain injury introduces a fundamentally different class of geometric

In TBI, tears occur.

Diffuse axonal injury (DAI) — the most common consequence of acceleration-

network.  In UM language, these cuts sever the information current
`J^μ_inf = φ² u^μ` along specific anatomical pathways.  This is not a
disruption of the field values — it is a change in the *topology* of the neural
manifold.  Paths that previously connected distant cortical regions are simply
gone.

Topological tears are irreversible in UM terms.  The winding numbers of the
original manifold cannot be continuously deformed back to their pre-injury
state once tearing has occurred.  Certain attractors `Ψ*_old` that were
supported by the original topology are geometrically inaccessible — not because
the brain cannot navigate to them, but because the topological structure that
defined them no longer exists.

### § 7.2 Concussion vs. Severe TBI: Distortion vs. Tearing

The UM framework provides a clear distinction between concussion (mild TBI) and
severe TBI:

**Concussion: metric distortion without topological tearing.**  
A concussive impact distorts `g_μν` — the metric becomes locally non-smooth,
signal propagation is disrupted, the holographic projection is temporarily
degraded.  But no axons are severed.  The topology is intact.  The fixed point
`Ψ*` still exists and can be reached — the geometry will reconverge once the
metric distortion clears.  This is why concussion symptoms typically resolve:
the brain is not in a new topology, it is in a temporarily distorted but
topologically identical manifold.

The UM analysis predicts that symptom persistence after concussion correlates
with residual metric non-smoothness (measurable in principle by DTI diffusion
tensor mapping) rather than with any change in topological structure.  Cognitive
rest allows metric recovery; early return to high-cognitive-load activities
before the metric has restabilised prolongs recovery by preventing the smooth
convergence back to `Ψ*`.

**Severe TBI: topological tearing.**  
When axons are severed, the topology changes.  The new manifold is genuinely
topologically distinct from the pre-injury manifold.  The brain will converge
to a new fixed point `Ψ*_new`, but `Ψ*_new ≠ Ψ*_original`.  This is a precise
geometric prediction of the personality change observed after severe TBI — not
just impairment of the old personality, but the emergence of a genuinely new
attractor state.  The changed person is not a degraded version of the original;
they are a different geometric object.

### § 7.3 Neuroplasticity as Topological Repair

The brain's neuroplastic capacity is its ability to form new winding pathways
around topological tears — to restore information current by routing around
severed connections.  Axonal sprouting, dendritic branching, and synaptogenesis
can partially restore the information current by creating new topological paths.

The UM analysis predicts that the endpoint of neuroplastic recovery is

## § 9 — Honest Boundaries

This document occupies the category the repository labels **STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE**.

mathematical framework applied to well-established neuroscience.

But there are three things this document is not:

The UM axioms are derived for cosmological field equations.  Extending them to
biological systems requires additional assumptions (that the brain's neural
manifold is governed by equations in the same mathematical class, that the
scaling of physical constants translates across 20 orders of magnitude from
cosmological to cellular scales).  These assumptions are not derived — they are
motivated by the structural correspondence.

medical advice.  Every specific prediction stated here would require a
well-powered, pre-registered clinical trial to validate.  The geometric
framework tells you *where to look* for effect; it does not tell you *whether
the effect exists at the magnitude needed for clinical utility*.

**3. It is not the only possible geometric framework.**  
The UM is one member of a family of 5D geometric theories.  Other frameworks
(Integrated Information Theory, predictive coding, active inference) share some
mathematical structure with the UM analysis.  Where they agree, the predictions
are reinforced.  Where they diverge, the disagreement identifies the specific
empirical question that would separate them.

These boundaries are stated not to diminish the framework's value, but because
a framework with honest edges is worth infinitely more than a framework with
inflated claims.  The correspondences documented here are real.  The
predictions that follow from them are specific.  The research agenda they
